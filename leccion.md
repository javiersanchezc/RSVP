# Lección de Pronunciación: Diálogos y Radio Dispatch
*Práctica de dicción, ritmo y entonación fluida.*

## Diálogo Policial (Radio Protocol)
1. [POLICE 1A] Dispatch, Unit 1A is on scene at 4th and Main Street.
2. [DISPATCH]: Copy that Unit 1A, proceed with caution.
3. [POLICE 1A] - Suspect vehicle is heading northbound on foot.
4. [DISPATCH] All available backup units are en route [10-4].
5. [POLICE 1A] Suspect has been safely detained, situation is under control.

## Conversación Diaria
6. [SPEAKER 1] Good morning! How have you been doing lately?
7. [SPEAKER 2]: I am doing very well, thank you for asking.
8. [SPEAKER 1] Consistent repetition builds muscle memory and vocal confidence.
